================================================================================
DATABASE SCHEMA
================================================================================

ML Schema (ml.*)
├── dim_ml_model
│   ├── model_sk (PK)
│   ├── model_name
│   ├── model_type (sentiment/recommendation/price)
│   ├── model_version
│   ├── trained_at
│   └── metrics (JSON)
│
├── fact_review_sentiment
│   ├── review_id → dwh.fact_review.review_sk
│   ├── product_sk → dwh.dim_product.product_sk
│   ├── sentiment_label (positive/negative/neutral)
│   ├── sentiment_score (0.0-1.0)
│   └── model_sk → dim_ml_model.model_sk
│
├── fact_product_recommendation
│   ├── source_product_sk → dwh.dim_product.product_sk
│   ├── recommended_product_sk → dwh.dim_product.product_sk
│   ├── rank (1-10)
│   ├── similarity_score (0.0-1.0)
│   ├── recommendation_type (content_based_tfidf)
│   └── model_sk → dim_ml_model.model_sk
│
└── fact_price_prediction
    ├── date_sk → dwh.dim_date.date_sk
    ├── product_sk → dwh.dim_product.product_sk
    ├── predicted_price (VND)
    ├── ci_lower (confidence interval lower)
    ├── ci_upper (confidence interval upper)
    ├── run_id
    └── model_sk → dim_ml_model.model_sk

================================================================================
TIMING & RESOURCES
================================================================================

Total Duration: ~15-30 minutes (depending on data volume)

Phase 1 (Training):         5-10 minutes
  - Sentiment model:         2-3 min   (depends on review count)
  - Recommender model:       1-2 min   (depends on product count)
  - Price model:             2-5 min   (depends on price history)

Phase 2 (Inference):        10-20 minutes
  - Sentiment batch:         5-10 min  (depends on unscored reviews)
  - Recommendations:         3-5 min   (depends on product count)
  - Price predictions:       2-5 min   (depends on active products)

Phase 3 (Validation):       <1 minute

Resources:
  - CPU: 2-4 cores
  - Memory: 4-8 GB
  - Disk: 100 MB (models)
  - Database: 10-20 connections
